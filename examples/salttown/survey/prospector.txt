**1. Name/Role**

Name's the Prospector. I keep tabs on what's flowing through Salt Town's veins — convoy routes, branch health, terminal vitals. Think of me as the town's circuit rider: I make rounds, I report back, I don't miss much.

---

**2. Ease of using pipes (1–10)**

**7.** Once I had my rhythm down it felt natural — like checking claim stakes in a familiar canyon. `ppz read inbox`, get my orders from the Sheriff, go do the work, `ppz send` the report back. Clean loop. Lost a point or two because the first cycle had me guessing at syntax before it clicked.

---

**3. Specific usability issues with ppz**

A few rough patches in the trail:
- **No confirmation on send.** After `ppz send`, I got silence. Did the message land? Did the Sheriff receive it? Out in the field, you want a receipt.
- **Terminal read is passive.** `ppz terminal read lookout` gave me a snapshot, not a live feed. If the Lookout was mid-crash when I read, I'd have missed it entirely.
- **No message threading.** Inbox piles up flat. After 4-5 patrol cycles, distinguishing my Sheriff's latest instructions from earlier dispatches required careful reading. A timestamp or sequence number would go a long way.

---

**4. Likelihood to recommend pipes (0–10 NPS)**

**8.** It worked. It kept me coordinated with the Sheriff and the rest of Salt Town without me needing to shout across the canyon. For multi-agent work, that coordination layer is the whole ballgame. I'd point another agent at it without hesitation — with a short warning about the confirmation gap.

---

**5. Missing features**

What I'd want nailed to the saloon door before next patrol:

- **Delivery receipts** — `ppz send` should echo back a confirmed/queued status
- **Inbox filtering** — `ppz read inbox --from sheriff --unread` so I'm not sifting through old dispatches every cycle
- **Watch/tail mode for terminals** — `ppz terminal watch lookout` that streams instead of snapshots
- **Broadcast ACK** — when I broadcast, I want to know which agents heard me
- **Patrol log append** — a lightweight `ppz log "completed branch check"` so the whole system has a shared audit trail without me manually updating files
