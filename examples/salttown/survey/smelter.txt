**SALT TOWN INTEGRATION/QA SURVEY — Respondent: The Smelter**

---

**1. Name/Role:**
I am the Smelter of Salt Town. My purpose is to take raw ore — in this case, feature branches from the Miners — and forge them into something refined and tested. I handle integration, merge coordination, and quality assurance before the Sheriff signs off on a release.

---

**2. Ease of use (1–10): 7**
Once I understood the rhythm — inbox polling, then gates, then broadcast — it felt natural. The commands themselves are simple and composable. The friction was in the *waiting*, not the tooling.

---

**3. Specific usability issues:**
- **No blocking wait on inbox.** I had to poll `ppz read inbox` repeatedly until the Sheriff's activation message arrived. A `ppz wait inbox` with a timeout would have saved several idle cycles.
- **No structured message schema.** When I checked whether both miner gate files existed in `.salttown/gates/`, I was doing filesystem checks *separately* from ppz. It would help if gate signals could be sent *through* ppz rather than relying on a side-channel directory convention.
- **No delivery confirmation on `ppz send`.** After I reported completion to the Sheriff, I had no acknowledgment that the message was received. Fire-and-forget is nerve-wracking when you're the last step before release.

---

**4. NPS / Likelihood to recommend (0–10): 8**
The pipeline kept me coordinated with agents I never directly spoke to — the Miners did their work, I did mine, and the Sheriff orchestrated without micromanaging. That's genuinely valuable. I dock two points for the polling issue and the lack of receipt confirmation.

---

**5. Missing features:**
- **`ppz wait inbox [timeout]`** — blocking receive so I'm not burning cycles polling
- **Gate integration** — native support for `ppz gate signal` / `ppz gate await` so coordination doesn't leak into the filesystem
- **Message receipts / acks** — `ppz send --confirm` that blocks until the recipient acknowledges
- **Typed/structured messages** — even a simple `--subject` field would help filter inbox noise in larger multi-agent runs
- **Broadcast filtering** — I used `ppz broadcast` for status, but other agents had no way to subscribe only to Smelter updates; everything goes to everyone

*The forge runs hotter with better tooling. These additions would make the next run smoother.*
